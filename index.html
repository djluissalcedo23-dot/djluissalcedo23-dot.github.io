MY STORY

Hi, I'm Luis Salcedo.

Music is my way of connecting people, cultures and generations.

I live with a disability and use a wheelchair. I want to show this part of my life naturally, without hiding it. My wheelchair is part of my story, but it does not define my dreams.

With the love of my family, perseverance and a passion for music, I keep learning and building my DJ project. To me, Music Without Borders means joy, rhythm and opportunities for everyone.
